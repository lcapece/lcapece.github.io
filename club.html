HOW A DOWN WORKS

PICK YOUR PLAY

You hold a hand of four play cards from a nine-play deck. Each card prints a target number. Want a fresh hand? That costs a real timeout.

THE DEFENSE CALLS ITS SHOT

The other coach reads your formation and commits — stop the run, cover short, or cover deep. Guess right and your target gets harder. Every down is a hand of poker.

TWO DICE DECIDE

Hit the target or better and the play connects — then the good cards throw again for yardage. Snake eyes is a catastrophe. Boxcars is a game-breaker. Seven is just Tuesday.

§2 · The felt

THE PROPOSITION LAYER

THE PROPOSITION CARDS

One blind card per team, per quarter. The payout grammar is honest proposition-bet math:

JACKPOT ~28% · CONSOLATION ~64% · BACKFIRE ~8%

A place bet doesn't pay nothing when it misses the number — it pays less. Win shapes vary like a real table: doubles, evens, a six on either die.

DEFENSIVE PROPOSITIONS

Jump the route. Send the house. Declared gambles the defense can take before the dice fly — decline them and the down resolves as if they never existed. The defense doesn't just guess. It can put money on the table.

SPECIAL TEAMS, AT REAL RATES — the coffin corner pins inside the five on 2, 3, 11, 12: a 16.7% shot, the actual NFL inside-the-ten rate. Punts go 38 + 2d6 — average 45, the real number. The onside kick recovers only on a rolled seven: seven out, the dice change hands.
