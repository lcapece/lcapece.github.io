Provably Fair Dice

The house cannot pick the dice it likes.

Mathematically verifiable integrity. The dice are the same for every coach. There is no statistical skewing. Coaching is the skill. Player cards change the choices you make, not the dice.

  1. Before kickoff the club seals a fingerprint of its dice plan — the brass seal at the coin toss.
  2. Your KICK IT OFF tap then cuts the deck, mixing your own timing in after the seal is already set.
  3. At the final gun the envelope opens, and VERIFY THIS GAME in the Binder re-rolls the entire game from it. Match, and the book is stamped VERIFIED — DICE TRUE.

The dice code is printed inside the game in full, and the build fails if it ever drifts from what shipped.
